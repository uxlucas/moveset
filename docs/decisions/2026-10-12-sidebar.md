# Sidebar direction

Date: 2026-10-12

## Inputs

- `/proto/sidebar` (removed after the decision): three variants behind a live picker. Rail (type only, dense), Context (only the current category, expanded), Outline (collapsible category tree).
- The owner's reference prototype, a single-page sidebar with per-term wireframe glyphs and scroll-spy.

## Decision

Rail's structure, with three things taken from the reference:

- Each group is a label plus a published count ("1 / 8", or just the total once the category is complete). No category icons and no Soon pills in the rail.
- Every term gets a 12px wireframe glyph. Only the current term's glyph moves. Drafts get a dashed frame instead.
- A highlight slides to the current row (300ms ease-out, none on first placement, off under reduced motion).
- On the index, scroll-spy picks the current card, so the rail tracks the reader's place in the dictionary.

About moves from the rail to a site footer.

## Rejected

- **Context**: hid the rest of the dictionary, and left an empty column on the index where no category is current.
- **Outline**: every other category cost a click to open, and the tree read as an app, not a reference.
- **A–Z rail and running head** (from the reference): the owner dislikes both.
- **Per-item Soon pills**: they wrap badly next to long names. The dashed glyph and faint text say the same thing in less room.
